1. Total Medicare Spend (by Service / Provider)
What it answers:
“How much money is Medicare spending?”
Definition:
Total Medicare payments aggregated by:
Provider
Service (procedure)
Region or place of service
Business value:
Identifies high-cost services
Highlights top-spending providers
Supports budgeting and cost containment discussions

2. Average Cost per Service
What it answers:
“How expensive is a single service on average?”
Definition:
Medicare Payments ÷ Number of Services
Business value:
Enables fair cost comparisons
Detects unusually expensive services
Helps assess pricing efficiency
