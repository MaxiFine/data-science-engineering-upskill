# CFO Action Plan: Financial Performance & Investment Priorities

## Executive Summary
- Performance is strongest and most consistent in Government and Small Business segments. Many lines show healthy profits and margins for products like `Paseo`, `Montana`, `Carretera`, and `VTT` in those segments.
- Enterprise deals frequently erode margin or turn negative, especially under High discount bands for `VTT`, `Velo`, and occasionally `Amarilla`/`Paseo`. Discount governance and price floors are required.
- Country-wise, both Canada and France contribute materially. Canada shows multiple high-profit Government entries (e.g., `Paseo`, `Amarilla`, `Carretera`); France also shows strong Government/Small Business performance (e.g., `Velo`, `Montana`, `VTT`). Focus investments where margins are stable and discounts are moderate.

## Investment Priorities
- Country: Double down in Canada and France, prioritizing Government and Small Business segments where margins are consistently positive.
- Products to scale:
  - `Paseo`: Broadly strong across Government/Small Business and Channel Partners in Canada.
  - `Montana`: Reliable in Government and Small Business, especially in France.
  - `Carretera`: Strong in Government; mixed elsewhere—selective focus.
  - `VTT`: Profitable in Government/Small Business; avoid Enterprise with High discounts.
- Products/contexts to constrain:
  - `Velo` and `VTT` in Enterprise under High discount bands (frequent negative margins).
  - Any SKU consistently sold at High discounts without volume/profit justification.

## Sales Growth Actions
- Pricing/Discount Governance:
  - Establish SKU × Segment price floors and an approval workflow for High discounts.
  - Tie discounts to profitable volume tiers and margin thresholds; cap High discount usage.
- ASP Uplift & Mix:
  - Rebalance toward Medium/Low discount bands; bundle high-margin accessories/services.
  - Prioritize upsell/cross-sell motions for `Paseo` and `Montana`.
- Channel Strategy:
  - Codify playbooks for Government/Small Business; replicate Canada’s Channel Partners success for `Paseo`.
  - Align partner incentives to margin, not just volume.

## Profit Increase & COGS Optimization
- Procurement & Supplier Strategy:
  - Consolidate suppliers on `VTT`/`Velo` components; negotiate volume contracts and hedges.
  - Target 2–5% COGS reduction over 2 quarters on low-margin SKUs.
- Engineering & Manufacturing:
  - Simplify BOM and redesign-for-cost on loss-prone SKUs; run value-engineering sprints.
  - Lean initiatives (setup time reduction, yield improvements) on high-volume lines.
- Logistics:
  - Mode optimization and nearshoring for high-demand regions; reduce freight and lead-time costs.
- Portfolio Management:
  - Sunset or reprice chronically negative Enterprise variants; shift marketing to proven SKU/segment mixes.

## Country × Product Actions
- Canada:
  - Government: Scale `Paseo`, `Amarilla`, `Carretera` where margins are strong.
  - Small Business: Expand `VTT`/`Montana` after confirming margin stability.
  - Enterprise: Restrict deals to price floors; avoid High discount band for `VTT`/`Velo`.
- France:
  - Government: Scale `Velo`, `Montana`, `VTT` with proven margins; audit `Paseo` where occasional negatives appear.
  - Channel Partners: Leverage `Carretera`/`VTT` performance; extend partner enablement.
  - Enterprise: Tighten discount controls; focus on SKUs with healthy unit economics.

## KPIs to Track (Weekly/Monthly)
- Gross Margin % by SKU × Segment × Country.
- Profit per Unit and Contribution Profit.
- Discount Rate distribution and ASP vs price floor by SKU.
- COGS per Unit and variance (procurement/manufacturing/logistics components).
- Mix of high-margin products (% of sales) and Segment profit share.
- Loss-making deal count and $ avoided via price/discount governance.
- Inventory turns, stockouts, and backorder rates.

## 90-Day Plan
- Weeks 0–2: Publish price floor policy; stand up dashboards for margin/discount guardrails; flag loss-prone deals pre-approval.
- Weeks 3–6: Supplier negotiations on `VTT`/`Velo`; pilot discount caps in Canada Government; review Enterprise pipeline for repricing.
- Weeks 7–12: Roll out pilots to France; train sales/partners on margin-first motions; update comp plans to reward profitable mix.

## Risks & Mitigations
- Government budget cycles: Build multi-year frameworks; diversify segments.
- Supplier lock-in: Add secondary sources; negotiate flexibility clauses.
- Enterprise churn from pricing discipline: Offer value-based bundles and ROI proofs.
