# Unpaid Experience – Protocol Member (Church)

Below are three copy-ready options you can use in the “Unpaid experience” section of your teacher training application. The first two are concise paragraphs (short and expanded). The third is a bullet version if the form prefers bullet points. Replace items in brackets to suit your case.

---

## Option A — Concise (≈550–650 chars)
As a volunteer Protocol member at [Church/Organisation], I welcome families, guide them to the children’s service, and support parents with infants. I supervise children to maintain a safe, calm environment, applying safeguarding awareness, clear communication, and positive behaviour strategies. I coordinate with leaders to manage smooth transitions and attendance. Commitment: [X hours/week], [Month Year–Present]. This role has strengthened my classroom management, duty of care, and inclusive practice with diverse learners.

## Option B — Expanded (≈900–1000 chars)
I volunteer as a Protocol member at [Church/Organisation], helping families on arrival and guiding them to the children’s service. I support parents with infants, direct footfall to appropriate spaces, and supervise children so worship remains safe and focused. The role requires safeguarding awareness, clear and friendly communication, and consistent behaviour expectations. I collaborate with the children’s ministry team to manage transitions, track attendance, and adapt to varying group sizes. I remain attentive to individual needs (including SEND), promote inclusion, and de-escalate minor issues using positive strategies. Since [Month Year], I have served [weekly/fortnightly] for about [X hours/week]. This experience has developed skills directly relevant to teaching: classroom management, safeguarding mindset, teamwork with leaders, and maintaining a purposeful learning environment.

## Option C — Bulleted responsibilities and impact
- Welcome families and signpost them to the children’s service; support parents with infants
- Supervise children to ensure safety, inclusion, and calm, orderly movement between spaces
- Apply safeguarding awareness and positive behaviour strategies; communicate clearly with children and adults
- Coordinate transitions and basic attendance tracking with the children’s ministry team
- Serve [weekly/fortnightly], ~[X hours/week], since [Month Year]
- Impact: strengthened classroom management, duty of care, and collaborative working with leaders

## Tips for the application form
- Keep sentences short and active. Avoid repetition (e.g., say “whilst/while,” not “whiles”).
- Emphasise skills directly relevant to teaching: safeguarding, behaviour, inclusion, communication, teamwork, reliability.
- Quantify commitment (hours/week, dates) to show consistency and responsibility.
- If possible, add one brief outcome (e.g., smoother transitions, improved calm in children’s area).
